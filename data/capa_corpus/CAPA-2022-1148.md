# CAPA-2022-1148

- **Component:** water_pump
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-03-19
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Water Pump — impeller vane erosion

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the water pump fail? Observed impeller vane erosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the engine-dress torque with automatic line stop.
