# CAPA-2022-1000

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-05-01
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — relief valve wear

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a bearing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed relief valve wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a bearing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at the final-assembly station.
