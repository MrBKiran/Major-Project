# CAPA-2024-1016

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-01-10
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — pickup screen blockage

## Root cause
A revised bearing specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed pickup screen blockage.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised bearing specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the bearing.
