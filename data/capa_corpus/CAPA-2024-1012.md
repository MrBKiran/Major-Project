# CAPA-2024-1012

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-03-02
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Radiator — core tube leak

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance housing press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed core tube leak.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance housing press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at the engine-dress station.
