# CAPA-2021-1036

- **Component:** brake_pads
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-12-19
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Brake Pads — backing plate corrosion

## Root cause
Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 110°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the brake pads fail? Observed backing plate corrosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 110°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the engine-dress station.
