# CAPA-2023-1102

- **Component:** battery
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-12-12
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Battery — plate sulphation

## Root cause
A revised O-ring specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the battery fail? Observed plate sulphation.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised O-ring specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
