# CAPA-2023-1058

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-05-05
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Radiator — fin corrosion

## Root cause
Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 110°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed fin corrosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 110°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
