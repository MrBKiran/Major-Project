# CAPA-2023-1061

- **Component:** water_pump
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-02-13
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Water Pump — impeller seal weep

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a bushing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the water pump fail? Observed impeller seal weep.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a bushing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line vibration-signature test at the leak-test station.
