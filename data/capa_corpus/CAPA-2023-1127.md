# CAPA-2023-1127

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-12-28
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Alternator — slip ring wear

## Root cause
Supplier changed the O-ring compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 105°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed slip ring wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the O-ring compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 105°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the O-ring.
