# CAPA-2024-1085

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-05-24
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — relief valve wear

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a bushing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed relief valve wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a bushing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the engine-dress torque with automatic line stop.
