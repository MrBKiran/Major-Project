# CAPA-2021-1067

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-07-24
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Radiator — core tube leak

## Root cause
Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed core tube leak.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the engine-dress torque with automatic line stop.
