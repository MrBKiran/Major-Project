# CAPA-2022-1118

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-12-13
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Radiator — core tube leak

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a shaft dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed core tube leak.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a shaft dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line vibration-signature test at the final-assembly station.
