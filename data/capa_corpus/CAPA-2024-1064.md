# CAPA-2024-1064

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-09-21
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — shaft bearing wear

## Root cause
Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed shaft bearing wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the sub-assembly station.
