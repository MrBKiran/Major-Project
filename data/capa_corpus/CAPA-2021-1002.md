# CAPA-2021-1002

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-05-15
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — raceway spalling

## Root cause
Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed raceway spalling.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the final-assembly torque with automatic line stop.
