# CAPA-2022-1137

- **Component:** battery
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-05-10
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Battery — plate sulphation

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance housing press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the battery fail? Observed plate sulphation.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance housing press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at the press-fit station.
