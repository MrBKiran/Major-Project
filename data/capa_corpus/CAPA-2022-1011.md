# CAPA-2022-1011

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-02-12
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — preload loss

## Root cause
Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed preload loss.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
