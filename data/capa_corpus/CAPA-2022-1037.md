# CAPA-2022-1037

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-02-02
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — wastegate sticking

## Root cause
Supplier changed the seal compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed wastegate sticking.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the seal compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the seal.
