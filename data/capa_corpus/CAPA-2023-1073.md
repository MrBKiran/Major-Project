# CAPA-2023-1073

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-12-05
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — raceway spalling

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance gasket press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed raceway spalling.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance gasket press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
