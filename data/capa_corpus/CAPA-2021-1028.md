# CAPA-2021-1028

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-03-21
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Alternator — slip ring wear

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance bearing press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed slip ring wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance bearing press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line vibration-signature test at the press-fit station.
