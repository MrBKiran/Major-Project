# CAPA-2023-1081

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-07-10
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Radiator — fin corrosion

## Root cause
Supplier changed the bearing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed fin corrosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the bearing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at the engine-dress station.
