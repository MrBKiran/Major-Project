# CAPA-2022-1108

- **Component:** brake_pads
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-05-28
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Brake Pads — premature friction material wear

## Root cause
Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the brake pads fail? Observed premature friction material wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the bushing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the bushing.
