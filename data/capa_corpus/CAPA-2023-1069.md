# CAPA-2023-1069

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-06-20
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — seal ingress

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed seal ingress.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the O-ring.
