# CAPA-2021-1057

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-01-17
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — relief valve wear

## Root cause
A revised bushing specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed relief valve wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised bushing specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
