# CAPA-2024-1035

- **Component:** clutch_assembly
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-05-01
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Clutch Assembly — release bearing noise

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a gasket dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the clutch assembly fail? Observed release bearing noise.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a gasket dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the sub-assembly torque with automatic line stop.
