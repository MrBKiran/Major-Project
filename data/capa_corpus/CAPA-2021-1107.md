# CAPA-2021-1107

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-01-22
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — pickup screen blockage

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance seal press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed pickup screen blockage.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance seal press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the seal.
