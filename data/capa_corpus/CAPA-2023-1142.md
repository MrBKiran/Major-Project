# CAPA-2023-1142

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-10-13
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Alternator — slip ring wear

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the final-assembly station drifted outside the control limit for 3 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed slip ring wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the final-assembly station drifted outside the control limit for 3 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the bearing.
