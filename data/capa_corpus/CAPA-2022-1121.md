# CAPA-2022-1121

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-03-07
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — rotor clearance drift

## Root cause
A revised O-ring specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed rotor clearance drift.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised O-ring specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the sub-assembly station.
