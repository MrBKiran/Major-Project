# CAPA-2024-1112

- **Component:** clutch_assembly
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-07-26
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Clutch Assembly — pressure plate distortion

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance shaft press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the clutch assembly fail? Observed pressure plate distortion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the press-fit station produced an out-of-tolerance shaft press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
