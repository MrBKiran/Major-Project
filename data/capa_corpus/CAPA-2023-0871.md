# CAPA-2023-0871

- **Component:** water_pump
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-WP
- **Opened:** 2023-03-14
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Impeller shaft seal weep leading to progressive coolant flow loss and overheat

## Root cause
Supplier substituted the impeller shaft seal compound from FKM to a lower-grade nitrile (NBR) for cost reasons. The NBR compound hardens above 105°C, losing conformity at the shaft interface and allowing coolant to weep past the bearing. Progressive flow loss then presents as a climbing coolant temperature on sustained highway load before the pump fails outright.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the engine overheat? Coolant flow dropped below the required rate.
2. Why did coolant flow drop? The water pump impeller lost sealing at the shaft.
3. Why did the seal fail? The seal compound hardened and lost conformity in service.
4. Why did the compound harden? NBR was substituted for FKM and degrades above 105°C.
5. Why was NBR substituted? A supplier cost-down change was accepted without high-temperature revalidation.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot; replace pump assembly and flush the cooling system on any unit showing weep at the bearing housing.

## Preventive action
Mandate FKM seal compound in the supplier specification and add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at 110°C to the water-pump station.
