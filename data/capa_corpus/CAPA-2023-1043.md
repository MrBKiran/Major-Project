# CAPA-2023-1043

- **Component:** brake_pads
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-08-12
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Brake Pads — premature friction material wear

## Root cause
A revised seal specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the brake pads fail? Observed premature friction material wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised seal specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the leak-test torque with automatic line stop.
