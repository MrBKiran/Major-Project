# CAPA-2022-1130

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-09-14
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Radiator — fin corrosion

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed fin corrosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the engine-dress torque with automatic line stop.
