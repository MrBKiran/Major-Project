# CAPA-2022-1052

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-03-23
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — relief valve wear

## Root cause
A revised shaft specification was released to production before the validation build completed.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed relief valve wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? A revised shaft specification was released to production before the validation build completed.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the final-assembly torque with automatic line stop.
