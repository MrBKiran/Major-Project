# CAPA-2024-1070

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-05-23
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Alternator — regulator failure

## Root cause
Supplier changed the shaft compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed regulator failure.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the shaft compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the press-fit torque with automatic line stop.
