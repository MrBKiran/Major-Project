# CAPA-2021-1019

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-02-22
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Alternator — slip ring wear

## Root cause
Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed slip ring wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the housing compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 120°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line flow test at the final-assembly station.
