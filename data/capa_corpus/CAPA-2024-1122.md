# CAPA-2024-1122

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-03-03
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — raceway spalling

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a shaft dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed raceway spalling.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a shaft dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
