# CAPA-2022-1082

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q4-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-04-12
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — shaft bearing wear

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a housing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed shaft bearing wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a housing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
