# CAPA-2024-1003

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-07-01
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — oil seal leak

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 8 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed oil seal leak.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 8 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the bushing.
