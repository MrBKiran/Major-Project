# CAPA-2022-1139

- **Component:** alternator
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-03-11
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Alternator — regulator failure

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 4 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the alternator fail? Observed regulator failure.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 4 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line vibration-signature test at the engine-dress station.
