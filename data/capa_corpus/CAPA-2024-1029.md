- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-06-10
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Water Pump — bearing play beyond limit

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the leak-test station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the water pump fail? Observed bearing play beyond limit.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the leak-test station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line flow test at the leak-test station.
