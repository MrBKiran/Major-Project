# CAPA-2022-1041

- **Component:** battery
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-07-01
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Battery — plate sulphation

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the leak-test station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the battery fail? Observed plate sulphation.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the leak-test station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the bearing.
