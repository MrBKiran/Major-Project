# CAPA-2024-1047

- **Component:** clutch_assembly
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-07-26
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Clutch Assembly — release bearing noise

## Root cause
Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 105°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the clutch assembly fail? Observed release bearing noise.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 105°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
