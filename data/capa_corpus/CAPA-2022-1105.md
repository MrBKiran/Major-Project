# CAPA-2022-1105

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-06-09
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Radiator — end tank crimp seep

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed end tank crimp seep.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a O-ring dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
