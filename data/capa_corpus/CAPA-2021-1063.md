# CAPA-2021-1063

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-07-21
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — pickup screen blockage

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a gasket dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed pickup screen blockage.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the leak-test station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a gasket dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
