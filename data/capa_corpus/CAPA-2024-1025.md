# CAPA-2024-1025

- **Component:** brake_pads
- **Plant:** Chakan
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-11-11
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Brake Pads — premature friction material wear

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the brake pads fail? Observed premature friction material wear.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the engine-dress station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the engine-dress station.
