# CAPA-2022-1100

- **Component:** battery
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-09-26
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Battery — terminal corrosion

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance seal press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the battery fail? Observed terminal corrosion.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the engine-dress station produced an out-of-tolerance seal press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the engine-dress station.
