# CAPA-2024-1086

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-01-12
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — wastegate sticking

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the press-fit station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed wastegate sticking.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the press-fit station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the press-fit station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
