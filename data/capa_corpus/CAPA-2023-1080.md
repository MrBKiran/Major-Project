# CAPA-2023-1080

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-10-03
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — seal ingress

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the sub-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed seal ingress.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the sub-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line vibration-signature test at the sub-assembly station.
