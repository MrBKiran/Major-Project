# CAPA-2022-1084

- **Component:** wheel_bearing
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2022-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2022-11-07
- **Status:** open

## Defect mode
Wheel Bearing — raceway spalling

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the wheel bearing fail? Observed raceway spalling.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance O-ring press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Rework the affected units at the regional service network.

## Preventive action
Add a dimensional gate at incoming inspection for the O-ring.
