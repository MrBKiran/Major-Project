# CAPA-2021-1119

- **Component:** battery
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q1-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-01-19
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Battery — low reserve capacity

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a bearing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the battery fail? Observed low reserve capacity.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the engine-dress station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a bearing dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line thermal-cycle test at the engine-dress station.
