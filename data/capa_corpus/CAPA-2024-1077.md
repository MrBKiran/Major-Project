# CAPA-2024-1077

- **Component:** oil_pump
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2024-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2024-04-01
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Oil Pump — pickup screen blockage

## Root cause
Assembly torque at the sub-assembly station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the oil pump fail? Observed pickup screen blockage.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Assembly torque at the sub-assembly station drifted outside the control limit for 7 weeks.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Contain stock at the plant and sort by supplier lot number.

## Preventive action
Add an end-of-line pressure-decay test at the sub-assembly station.
