# CAPA-2023-1144

- **Component:** radiator
- **Plant:** Pithampur
- **Batch:** BATCH-2023-Q2-STD
- **Opened:** 2023-11-08
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Radiator — end tank crimp seep

## Root cause
Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance bearing press fit.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the radiator fail? Observed end tank crimp seep.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Tooling wear at the final-assembly station produced an out-of-tolerance bearing press fit.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Quarantine and inspect all affected units in the batch.

## Preventive action
Revise the supplier specification and add a lot-level acceptance test.
