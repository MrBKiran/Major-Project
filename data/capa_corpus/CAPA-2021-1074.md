# CAPA-2021-1074

- **Component:** turbocharger
- **Plant:** Haridwar
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-08-14
- **Status:** closed

## Defect mode
Turbocharger — oil seal leak

## Root cause
Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the turbocharger fail? Observed oil seal leak.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the final-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Supplier changed the gasket compound without revalidation; the substitute hardens above 95°C.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the final-assembly torque with automatic line stop.
