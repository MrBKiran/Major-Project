# CAPA-2021-1039

- **Component:** fuel_injector
- **Plant:** Nashik
- **Batch:** BATCH-2021-Q3-STD
- **Opened:** 2021-04-15
- **Status:** monitoring

## Defect mode
Fuel Injector — spray pattern deviation

## Root cause
Incoming inspection sampling missed a seal dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.

## 5-Why
1. Why did the fuel injector fail? Observed spray pattern deviation.
2. Why did that occur? Deviation traced to the sub-assembly station.
3. Why was the deviation present? Incoming inspection sampling missed a seal dimensional deviation from a single supplier lot.
4. Why was it not detected? The existing control plan had no gate for this characteristic.
5. Why was there no gate? The FMEA did not rank this failure mode above the action threshold.

## Corrective action
Recall-inspect the affected lot and replace the component under warranty.

## Preventive action
Introduce SPC monitoring on the sub-assembly torque with automatic line stop.
